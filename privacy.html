Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026. Lumio (formerly ClinicGuard Personal) is an early-stage student project. This page says plainly what we keep and where.

Stored only on your device

  • Your tasks, steps, session times, reflections and progress, in your browser's local storage.
  • Clearing your browser data erases them. Use Export my data on the Account page to keep a copy.

Stored on our servers (Neon database, hosted with Vercel)

  • If you create an account: your email, a hashed password (scrypt; we can't read it), your plan, the date and count of today's AI actions, and login sessions.
  • Usage counts: event names like "session started" or "task finished", a random id stored in your browser, your account id if logged in, and the time. Never your task text, titles or anything you type.
  • AI usage: how many tokens each AI request used, for cost tracking. Not the content.
  • Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs (such as IP address) for security.

Lumio Together (only if you invite or accept a buddy)

  • Stored: the first name you choose, your sharing choices, the link between you and your buddy, invite links (only a scrambled hash, never the link itself), and short signals like "saw you show up" or "still there?".
  • While sharing is on: whether you're focusing right now and when that session started and ends. Turning sharing off deletes it immediately.
  • Your buddy never sees: your tasks, steps, notes, reflections, AI text or history. Those never reach our servers.
  • You can pause sharing, go solo or disconnect at any time. Deleting your account removes your buddy link and signals.

Sent to third parties

  • Anthropic (Claude): only when you tap an AI button, we send that task or step text to get a response. We don't store it.
  • Stripe: handles payments. We never see your card number. We store which checkout session paid for your account.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data or show ads.
  • We can't see, block or monitor other apps or websites. During a session, Lumio only notices that you left its own tab.

Your choices

  • Use Lumio without an account.
  • Export or erase your device data any time (Account page).
  • Delete your account permanently from the Account page. Usage counts linked to it are kept only in anonymous form.

Questions: contact the founder through the Penn State course or the GitHub repository.
