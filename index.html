      How a breach check stays private

      We use a technique called k-anonymity. The database only ever sees a tiny piece of a scrambled code shared by hundreds of other passwords.

      FAQ

      Good questions

      Where is my data stored?

      In your browser's local storage on this device. There's no ClinicGuard account or server database. The only thing that ever leaves your device is the 5-character piece of a scrambled password, and only when you press "Check privately."

      Is the first-step feature AI?

      Not yet. Today it's a built-in set of rules that works offline, so nothing about your tasks is sent anywhere. An optional AI version is on the roadmap, and it will always be your choice to turn it on.

      How does the device update check work?

      Phones and browsers don't let websites look inside your device, which is good for your privacy. So you tell ClinicGuard when each device was last updated, and it reminds you when it's overdue. We can also detect what device you're on right now to save you typing.

      What happens if I clear my browser data?

      Your planner is erased too, because it only lives here. Use "Export my data" in the My data tab to keep a backup file.

      Who's building this?

      ClinicGuard is a student startup from Penn State, built by a cybersecurity student. This is an early version, and we'd love your feedback.
