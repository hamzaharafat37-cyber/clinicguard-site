How it works

Three steps. That's the whole app.

1

Name the thing

Type what you're avoiding, in your own words. You get one tiny first step you can do in under 2 minutes, plus the next few.

2

Do one step at a time

Start a 5, 10 or 25 minute session. Only the current step is on screen. Stuck? One tap makes it smaller.

3

Drift? Come back.

Got distracted or left the tab? Comeback brings you back to the exact step you were on, with a fresh short timer. No lecture.

Why not just ask a chatbot?

FAQ

Questions

Does Comeback block apps or watch my phone?

No. Comeback is a website. It can't see, block or monitor other apps or sites. During a session it only notices when you leave its own tab, so it can help you get back. A browser extension is in development; it will only act on sites you choose, and you can turn it off any time.

What's stored, and where?

On your device: your tasks, steps and session history. On our servers (only with an account): your email, a hashed password, your plan, today's AI count, and anonymous usage counts like "a session started" (never what you typed). Sent to Anthropic (Claude): only the task or step text you send when you tap an AI button. See the privacy page.

Do I need an account?

No. Quick first steps, sessions and recovery work without one. A free account adds AI breakdowns written for your exact task.

What happened to ClinicGuard?

Comeback is the new name for ClinicGuard Personal. We focused on the one thing people told us they needed: actually starting. The old planner and security tools are kept in Labs.

Who's building this?

A Penn State student startup. It's early, and we'd love your feedback.
