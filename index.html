FAQ

Questions

What exactly does my buddy see?

Only that you started a session (if you turn sharing on), that you're focusing right now, and a simple "showed up today." Never your task, steps, notes, reflections or AI text. Those never even reach our servers. You can pause sharing, go solo or disconnect at any time.

Do I need a buddy?

No. Lumio works fully on your own. A buddy is a nice extra, never a requirement, and they never need to be online.

Will my buddy spam me?

They can send one gentle "Still there? You've got this." at most every 30 minutes, and you can turn encouragement off completely.

What happens when I miss something?

Nothing moves on its own. Missed items wait for you to choose: it happened, do 5 minutes now, move it, or drop it. Every change has an Undo.

What's stored, and where?

On your device: tasks, steps, sessions and reflections. On our servers (with an account): email, hashed password, plan, first name, sharing settings, whether you're focusing (only while sharing with a buddy), and anonymous usage counts. Sent to Anthropic (Claude): only the task text you send when you ask for AI help. See the privacy page.

Does Lumio block apps?

No. It's a website, so it can't see or block other apps. A browser extension is in development and will only act on sites you choose.

What happened to ClinicGuard?

Lumio is the new name for ClinicGuard Personal. The old tools are kept in Labs.
